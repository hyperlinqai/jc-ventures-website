Unlock Essential Government Services Through JC Ventures' Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) Network

As a Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) under JC Ventures, you can help customers access government schemes like Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (JJBY), Suraksha Bima Yojana (SBY) and Atal Pension Yojana (APY) in all Rural & Suburbans areas.
