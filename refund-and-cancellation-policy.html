Refund & Cancellation Policy

Information on reversals, cancellations, and refunds for JC Ventures services.

Last updated on: 28-09-2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how refunds, reversals and cancellations are handled for services availed through JC Ventures Pvt. Ltd. (“JC Ventures”, “we” or “us”), www.jcventures.in and its network of Customer Service Points (CSPs). It should be read with our Terms & Conditions and the terms of the applicable bank, insurer, lender, travel provider or other service provider.

1. Banking Transactions

Banking services at CSPs are provided by the partner bank, which holds your account and transaction records. Refunds for banking transactions are therefore processed by the bank.

  • If your account is debited but the transaction fails, for example a failed cash withdrawal or fund transfer, the applicable bank will process the reversal within the timelines prescribed by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and other applicable rules. Where compensation is prescribed for delay, it will be handled by the responsible bank or payment system in accordance with the applicable rules.
  • Once a banking transaction has been successfully processed, JC Ventures or the CSP generally cannot cancel or reverse it. Any reversal or recovery is subject to the applicable bank, payment system and regulatory procedures.
  • A transfer made to a wrong account because of incorrect details generally cannot be reversed by the CSP or JC Ventures. You should contact the bank immediately and request assistance for recovery, subject to the bank’s procedures and the recipient bank’s cooperation.
  • Always collect a receipt for every transaction. It will help the bank and JC Ventures trace the transaction if there is any issue.

2. Extra Charges Collected by a CSP

CSPs may collect only the charges approved by the applicable bank or relevant service provider. If a CSP collects any amount beyond approved charges or money for an unauthorised purpose, please report it through our complaint portal. We will investigate and, where the complaint is found valid and the amount was collected by the CSP, take reasonable steps to have the amount refunded and take appropriate action against the CSP.

3. Loans

Loan applications are decided by the bank or lender. Any processing fee or other charge is levied by the lender and any refund is subject to the lender’s applicable policy and terms. JC Ventures does not charge any fee for submitting a loan application.

4. Insurance

Cancellation and refund of insurance policies are governed by the insurer’s policy terms and applicable law, including any free-look period where applicable. Requests are forwarded to the insurer or relevant intermediary, and refunds, if any, are processed according to their decision and applicable rules. For an unresolved insurance grievance, policyholders may also use the insurer’s grievance process and, where applicable, IRDAI’s Bima Bharosa portal at bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in.

5. Travel Bookings

For travel bookings where offered, cancellations, refunds, rescheduling, no-show charges and other fees are governed by the applicable airline, railway, hotel, transport operator or other supplier terms. Any refund received by JC Ventures from the relevant supplier will be passed on to the customer after applicable deductions, taxes or disclosed service charges, where applicable. Processing time will depend on the supplier and payment channel.

6. Financial Literacy Programmes

Financial literacy workshops and training sessions are free of charge. No refund is applicable.

7. For CSPs

  • Any set-up fee paid by a CSP is non-refundable once the CSP is activated, unless the CSP agreement or disclosed fee terms provide otherwise. If the application is rejected, any fee stated as refundable in the applicable terms will be refunded in accordance with those terms.
  • Commission credited on a transaction that is later reversed by the bank will also be reversed, in accordance with the applicable CSP agreement.

8. How to Raise a Refund Request

For banking transactions, please contact your bank branch or the bank’s customer care. You may also raise the issue through our complaint portal at complaint.jcventures.in, by email to info@jcventures.in or by calling +91 7470785826, and we will help you take it up with the bank. Please share the transaction receipt, date, amount and CSP details.

We will acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours and endeavour to provide a response or status update within 15 days of receipt. Where final resolution depends on a bank, insurer, lender, travel provider or other third party, the final timeline will depend on that provider and the applicable process. If a banking complaint is not resolved within the applicable period under the bank’s grievance process or RBI framework, or you are not satisfied with the bank’s response, you may use the RBI grievance mechanism, including the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme where applicable, at cms.rbi.org.in.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated Policy will be posted on the Website with a revised “Last updated” date.
