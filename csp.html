Case Study 1: Empowering Rural Banking in Bihar

Story: Rajesh Kumar transformed his small village into a hub for banking services, providing locals with easy access to essential financial services. His Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) has enabled hundreds of villagers to open bank accounts, apply for loans, and receive insurance.

Impact: Increased financial inclusion, improved local economy, and enhanced community trust.

- Rajesh Kumar, Bihar

Case Study 2: Bringing Financial Services to Remote Areas

Story: Sunita Devi established her Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) in a remote area, bridging the gap between financial institutions and her community. Her dedication has helped many people with transactions, savings, and educational loans.

Impact: Expanded access to financial services, promoted economic stability, and supported educational growth.

- Sunita Devi, Uttar Pradesh

Case Study 3: Enhancing Access to Financial Literacy

Story: Manoj Patel’s Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) is not only a banking service point but also a center for financial literacy. By conducting workshops and training, he has empowered his community with valuable financial knowledge.

Impact: Increased financial awareness, better money management, and greater community engagement.

-Manoj Patel, Gujarat
