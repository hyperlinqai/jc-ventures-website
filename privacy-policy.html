Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we safeguard your information.

Last Updated: July 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

JC Ventures collects information you provide directly to us when filling out our contact form, requesting information, or corresponding with our team. This includes your name, email address, phone number, and any project details submitted.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests;
  • Evaluate partnership, funding, or advisory proposals;
  • Send administrative notices, security updates, and relevant corporate announcements;
  • Improve the functionality and security of our website.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies

Our website uses standard cookies to enhance user navigation and analyze traffic patterns. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data stored by JC Ventures. To make a request, contact us at info@jcventures.in.
