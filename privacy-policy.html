Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we safeguard your information.

Last updated on: 28-09-2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JC Ventures Pvt. Ltd. (“JC Ventures”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, stores and protects personal data through www.jcventures.in, its CSP login and complaint portals (the “Website”), and its network of Customer Service Points (CSPs) (together, the “Services”). It should be read with our Terms & Conditions. This Policy explains our data practices; where consent is required, we will seek it through the relevant notice, form or other lawful mechanism.

This Policy is intended to be read with the Information Technology Act, 2000, applicable rules, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, to the extent the relevant provisions are in force and applicable to JC Ventures.

1. Who This Policy Covers

  • CSPs and applicants who apply for or operate a CSP; and
  • Visitors to the Website, including anyone who submits an enquiry or complaint.

Customers of partner banks: Customer banking data collected at a CSP, such as KYC documents, Aadhaar, biometrics and account details, is entered directly into the partner bank’s systems where the relevant bank workflow requires this and is primarily held by the bank. JC Ventures does not intentionally store such bank-held customer KYC or biometric data in its own systems where it is not required for the service. Any limited operational, reconciliation, complaint or technical information processed by JC Ventures is governed by this Policy.

2. Data We Collect

From CSPs and applicants: name, contact details, KYC documents, photographs, educational and address details, police or background verification reports, shop and location details, bank account details for commission payments, and CSP transaction summaries (such as reference number, amount and date) needed for commission calculation, reconciliation, support, security or compliance.

From Website visitors and complainants: name, contact details and messages submitted through forms or the complaint portal, together with technical data such as IP address, device and browser details and cookie-related information, where collected.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data only for purposes connected with the Services and our lawful business operations, including the following:

  • Onboard, verify, train and manage CSPs and pay their commission;
  • Monitor CSP operations and prevent fraud, cash shortfalls, misuse and security incidents;
  • Handle queries, service requests and complaints;
  • Conduct financial literacy programmes and provide related communications;
  • Comply with RBI requirements, applicable anti-money laundering and tax laws, contractual obligations and lawful requests from authorities;

4. Aadhaar and Biometric Data

Customer Aadhaar and biometric data captured through an applicable bank or authorised authentication workflow is processed according to the bank’s and authorised authentication provider’s requirements and is not intentionally stored by JC Ventures where the workflow does not require JC Ventures to store it. Aadhaar details of CSPs and applicants may be collected where required for KYC or verification, with the required consent or other lawful basis. Full Aadhaar numbers are not intentionally stored except where permitted or required by law.

5. Consent

We process personal data on the basis of consent where consent is required, and on other lawful bases or permissions where permitted by applicable law, including for contracts, compliance, fraud prevention, security and regulatory obligations. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent using the same or a comparably easy channel used to give it, or by contacting us through the details in Section 15. Withdrawal will not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal, and we may be unable to continue some Services where processing is necessary.

6. Sharing of Data

We do not sell personal data. We share personal data only where necessary, lawful and proportionate for the relevant purpose, including with:

  • Partner banks, for CSP onboarding, approval and authorised services;
  • Verification agencies, for KYC and background checks of CSPs and applicants;
  • Service providers supporting our operations, such as technology, hosting, communications and SMS providers, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security requirements;
  • Our group companies, where necessary to provide, administer or improve the Services and where permitted by law; and
  • Regulators, courts, tax authorities, law enforcement agencies and other persons where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

7. Duties of CSPs

CSPs must enter customer data only into the systems and applications authorised by the applicable bank. They must not keep copies of customer documents or data, record it elsewhere, photograph or share it without authorisation, and must keep their devices and login credentials secure.

8. Data Storage and Security

Personal data held by us is currently stored in India. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, encryption or other appropriate protections, monitoring and periodic review, to protect personal data. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will take the steps required by applicable law, including notifying affected persons and authorities where required.

9. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to perform contracts, resolve disputes, maintain business and audit records, or comply with applicable legal, regulatory, tax, banking and anti-money laundering requirements. Different categories of CSP, transaction, complaint and technical records may therefore have different retention periods. When retention is no longer required, data is deleted, anonymised or otherwise disposed of securely, subject to applicable law.

10. Cookies

The Website uses cookies and similar technical tools to keep the Website functioning, support security and understand how the Website is used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

11. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and the commencement of the relevant provisions, you may have rights to:

  • Request information or a summary about the personal data we hold and how it is used;
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Ask us to erase personal data where applicable, unless we must retain it under law or for another lawful purpose;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • Nominate a person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity, where provided by applicable law; and
  • Raise a grievance with us and, where applicable after using our grievance process, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Requests relating to your bank account, banking KYC, account transactions or other data held by a partner bank should generally be made directly to that bank. We will assist where the matter relates to data processed by JC Ventures.

12. Children

The Website is intended for persons aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the Website. Where a service involves a child’s data under applicable law, any required parental consent and safeguards will be followed.

13. Third-Party Websites

The Website may contain links to other websites, including those of partner banks, service providers and group companies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. Please review their privacy policies before providing personal data to them.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated Policy will be posted on the Website with a revised “Last updated” date. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels where required by law.
