Simple Steps to Access Essential Services

Our Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) make it easy: visit your local kiosk, get assistance with account setup or transactions, and access a range of financial services with ease. Experience straightforward, reliable service every time.

Registration:

  • Visit your nearest Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) with the necessary documents.
  • The Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) operator will help you fill out the registration form and capture your biometric data.
  • Once registered, you will receive a confirmation message.

Account Opening:

  • Provide the required identification documents to the Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) operator.
  • Your account details will be processed, and you will receive an account number.
  • You can start using your account for deposits, withdrawals, and fund transfers.

Applying for Loans:

  • Discuss your loan requirements with the Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) operator.
  • Submit the necessary documents and complete the application form.
  • The Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) operator will forward your application to the bank for approval.
  • Upon approval, the loan amount will be credited to your account.

Insurance Enrollment:

  • Choose the insurance plan that best suits your needs.
  • Provide the required details and complete the enrollment form.
  • The Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC) operator will process your application, and you will receive your policy documents.

Participating in Financial Literacy Programs:

  • Check the schedule for upcoming workshops and training sessions at your Customer Service Point (CSP) / Business Correspondents (BC).
  • Register for the sessions that interest you.
  • Attend the sessions to gain valuable financial knowledge and skills.
